Projects

Production · Korean news broadcaster

Live broadcast translation

Read case study →

Problem

Live Korean-to-English news subtitles needed BLEU 30 or higher, fast enough for live use. The starting model scored 16.5.

What I built

Compared 10+ translation models and LLMs, fine-tuned Gemma with LoRA and traditional translation models (M2M100, NLLB), and added an LLM correction step.

Result

BLEU 16.5 → 30.8 on a news test set, above the target of 30, at about 27 ms per sentence on one GPU.

Consumer app · 500K+ downloads, acquired

KpopPro: LyricSync and AI Cover

Read case study →

Problem

The app needed lyrics synced to songs and covers sung in the user's own voice. The alignment model only handled English, but K-pop is sung mostly in Korean.

What I built

The full lyrics pipeline, Korean support for the alignment model by fine-tuning it on Korean singing, and singing voice conversion that adapts to each user.

Result

94.9% alignment accuracy in Korean, against 95.2% in English. The app reached 500K+ downloads and was acquired.

B2B · Major Korean mobile carrier

Audio source separation

Listen to samples →

Problem

Real audio mixes speech with crowd noise, music and background sound, which breaks speech recognition, subtitles and dubbing.

What I built

Separation models for vocals vs. music and speech vs. noise, compared against off-the-shelf denoisers on real broadcast audio.

Result

Speech recognition accuracy rose from 91% on the raw audio to 96% on the separated audio, which helped win a contract with a major Korean mobile carrier.

Production · Korean university

University lectures, subtitled and dubbed

Read case study →

Problem

A Korean university needed 7 of its courses (100+ hours of lectures) in English. Speech recognition and translation get technical course terms wrong.

What I built

A pipeline that uses the lecture slides to get each course's vocabulary right in speech recognition and translation, then dubs in a clone of each lecturer's voice.

Result

7 courses, 100+ hours delivered in English, with a confidence score on every subtitle so human review goes only where needed.
