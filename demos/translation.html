Live Broadcast Translation

A Korean news broadcaster needed live Korean-to-English subtitles. I built the translation stage. The target was BLEU 30 or higher, fast enough for live use. The model we started from scored 16.5.

What I did

  • Compared more than 10 translation models and LLMs.
  • Fine-tuned Gemma with LoRA, and fine-tuned traditional translation models (M2M100, NLLB).

Results

Task Before After
News translation, BLEU on a news test set 16.5 30.8

The news system runs at about 27 ms per sentence on one GPU.

Separately, on a medical interpreting test set (dermatology and plastic surgery conversations), training the model to follow a glossary raised medical term accuracy from 43.9% to 80.3%, beating an LLM that is about 7x slower.

What the experiments showed

  • Filtering training data with an automatic quality score did no better than picking sentences at random.
  • Adding LLM-generated synthetic data made the model worse (−2.5 BLEU), while adding real spoken data helped (+2 BLEU).
