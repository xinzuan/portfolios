KpopPro: LyricSync and AI Cover

I built the two main AI features of STRA's KpopPro app: LyricSync, which shows K-pop lyrics in time with the song, and AI Cover, which lets users hear a song sung in their own voice. The app reached 500K+ downloads, placed 3rd in the Google for Startups Changgoo Program and was later acquired.

LyricSync

  • Built the full lyrics pipeline: finding the lyrics, detecting where singing happens, and aligning each line to the moment it is sung.
  • The alignment model only handled English, but K-pop is sung mostly in Korean. I added Korean by building a Korean pronunciation pipeline and fine-tuning the model on Korean singing.
  • Built an English and Korean test set for singing, since speech models are trained mostly on speech and singing is much harder for them.

Results

Task English Korean
Lyrics alignment (word starts within 0.5 s) 95.2% 94.9%
Lyrics transcription error 8.1% WER 13.5% CER

Korean ended up almost as accurate as English, even though the model originally could not handle Korean at all.

AI Cover

  • Built singing voice conversion that adapts to each user: a user records their voice, and the app creates a cover of a song in that voice while keeping the melody.
  • Also adopted by Korea Job World for its education and career exploration programs.
