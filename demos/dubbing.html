AI Dubbing & Voice Cloning

A dubbing platform supporting 20+ languages that dubs video in the original speaker's own voice. For how it handled university lectures, see the lecture project.

What I did

  • Built the AI pipeline end to end on open-source and open-weight models: speech recognition, LLM translation, speech generation and voice cloning.
  • Built automatic voice matching, which picks the library voice closest to each speaker so the final voice conversion sounds more natural.
  • Built quality checks into the pipeline. A line too long for its time slot is re-translated shorter rather than sped up, because a sped-up voice sounds unnatural. The finished dub is transcribed again line by line, and any missing or wrong line is re-generated before delivery.
  • Chose the voice engine by blind-testing 22 voices from 10 text-to-speech engines.

Results

  • Supports 20+ languages, and was used to dub 7 courses (100+ hours) for a Korean university.
  • In testing, the chosen engine said 96% of the script correctly.
