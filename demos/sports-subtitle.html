Soccer Subtitles

A sports media client needed English and Korean subtitles for soccer content: match commentary, pre- and post-match shows, and club magazines. I built a system that produces them automatically, which professional editors then review before delivery.

Results

  • About 500 videos (400+ hours) subtitled for a sports media client.
  • Professional editors kept over 90% of the AI's Korean translation.

How it works

  • Separates the commentators' voices from crowd noise and stadium sound before speech recognition, so loud moments don't turn into invented text.
  • Checks player and team names against a squad list, because names are the errors viewers notice first.
  • Translates into Korean in four styles, from literal to broadcast-natural, so the client can pick what fits each show.
  • Runs a quality check on both the transcript and the translation before anything goes to the editors.
