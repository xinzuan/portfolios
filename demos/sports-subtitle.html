Sports Subtitle Pipeline

End-to-end subtitle system tailored for sports broadcasts. Handles noisy stadium audio, crowd/speech separation, player name recognition, and multilingual translation with domain-specific quality control. 10+ versions iterated with different ASR engines, denoising strategies, and QC approaches. Users can select from multiple pipeline versions, each optimized for different content characteristics (e.g., long-form broadcasts, highlight clips, noisy stadium environments).

Example: Manchester City vs Arsenal — 2026 Carabao Cup Final

Pre-match broadcast commentary processed through the pipeline. Below shows real outputs across the pipeline stages: audio enhancement, version iteration, transcription QC, and translation with multi-stage QC.

Audio: Source Separation & Enhancement

The original broadcast audio contains crowd noise, stadium ambience, and background music mixed with commentary speech. Our source separation and speech enhancement pipeline isolates the speech channel for cleaner ASR input.
